🚨 Stop the Attack — Mission 5

If you spot a message like this, what should you do?

  • ✅ Don't click suspicious links
  • ✅ Verify through a trusted channel (call or message the sender directly)
  • ✅ Report the message to IT / Security
  • ✅ Use Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) on your account
  • ❌ Enter your credentials into a suspicious page
Even if a password is stolen, MFA adds a second step that can stop the attacker from getting in.
